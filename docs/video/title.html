Serverpod as a Zenoh peer

A Serverpod server on the device network, in its own process.

A phone publishes its sensors over Zenoh; the server keeps a vibration level per second and opens two doors: Serverpod endpoints for browsers, a Zenoh query and publication for native apps.

Hugo Alberto Garcia, Bluecorn · Build Something Real, the Serverpod hackathon, October 2026
